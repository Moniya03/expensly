# Expensly Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 3 August 2026

This policy explains what data Expensly collects, why, and your rights. By using Expensly you agree to this policy.

## 1. What we collect

| Data | Purpose | Stored where |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email (Google sign-in) | Identify you in the app | Supabase (Mumbai, India) |
| Transactions you log (amount, category, note, date) | Core app function | Supabase |
| Budgets, savings goals, splits | Core app function | Supabase |
| Voice recordings (optional) | Transcribe expense descriptions | Sent to OpenAI Whisper for transcription, then deleted; only the text is kept |
| Bug reports + attachments you submit | Improve the app | Supabase storage |
| App feedback ratings | Improve the app | Supabase |

## 2. How we use it

- To run the app's features (tracking, goals, budgets)
- To transcribe voice notes into expense entries
- To respond to bug reports and feedback
- To improve the product

We never sell your data. We never show ads.

## 3. Voice data

When you use voice input, your audio is sent to OpenAI's Whisper service for transcription. The audio is processed for transcription only and is not retained by us. Your email address is not shared with OpenAI.

## 4. Data storage & security

Data is stored on Supabase servers (Mumbai region) with row-level security: each user can only read/write their own data. Authentication is handled by Google sign-in.

## 5. Your rights

- **Delete your account:** in the app go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, transactions, budgets, goals, splits, feedback, and bug reports.
- **Export your data:** contact us (below) and we will provide a copy of your data.
- **Withdraw consent:** deleting your account stops all collection.

## 6. Children

Expensly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above reflects the latest version.
